# The Research Brief page: the one file Ryan reads (standard since 2026-10-29)

Ryan, 2026-10-29, on `offers/osteoshield/RESEARCH-BRIEF.html`: "so good that I want you to save it and do it for all of
them moving forward. It could actually be more in-depth, more fire, with genius little ideas that you put in here,
more of that shit."

So: every offer's research ends in ONE page, `offers/<slug>/RESEARCH-BRIEF.html`, built the way the OsteoShield one
is built. The per-source docs under `research/` stay the receipts. The page is what he opens on his phone. Copy the
OsteoShield file's CSS exactly (one column, numbered sections, cards for ranked items, tables scroll sideways, dark
mode works). Change the words, not the shape.

## What the page is

Three reads in one page, then the numbers, then the ideas: who she is, what the VSL gives us, what the market is
doing. Every section is built from the counted data in `research/`. A line with no number behind it does not go on
the page. Plain words, fourth-grade reading level, the client's own phrases in quotes.

## The sections, in order

0. **Head.** Offer name, client, pull date. Four numbers in a row: posts read, client ads in Atria, advertisers
   dissected plus videos transcribed, angle seeds pulled from the VSL. Real counts, no rounding up.

1. **The buyer.** One named woman with an age and a house (Linda, 71, stairs to the laundry). Four cards: biggest
   pain, biggest fear, what she has tried, the win she believes. Then the pains table, ranked by how often each
   repeats, with the count and her exact words next to each. Then one line on what she does NOT say (grandkids came
   up twice in 3,485 posts).

2. **The VSL, reverse-engineered.** Length, who narrates, how close the script is to the control. The mechanism
   stack as four cards: problem, solution, enemy, stories. "Proof points worth carrying" as a short list. "Open
   loops to watch": the places where the ad promise and the VSL do not match, and the lines the VSL says that no ad
   has used. The strongest line in the script that is in no ad gets its own call-out.

3. **The market (Atria).** Who is spending: a table with ads, live, funnel type, and each player's "real reason"
   (their mechanism in five words). Top outcomes ranked with who leads with each. Top personas with the exact
   hook that proves each one. Which speaker wins. Patterns, as short bullets with counts.

4. **Gaps.** The table Ryan reads twice: she talks about it (with the post count) and nobody runs it (what the
   market does instead). This is where the campaigns come from.

5. **More rankings from the same data.** Every count the scrape gave us, one line each, biggest first, and under
   each one a **Learning:** line in plain words. The OsteoShield page has twelve of these: drugs she names, where it
   actually broke, how she feels in her own word, what her doctor told her, daily life she names, what she blames,
   what she tries instead of the drug, the questions she asks, her scan number, her age, the "X vs Y" searches,
   what the reviews talk about, the top upvoted posts. Do all that apply, plus any the offer invents.

6. **Flags for the client.** Three to five facts the client would want before spending: the page their ads run
   from, the review a skeptic finds first, the swipe they asked for that does not exist.

## The upgrade (what "more fire" means)

7. **Keyword bank.** New since 2026-10-29. The nouns she actually types, ranked by count, in one table: drug names,
   scan words, body parts, places, feelings, what she tries. Ryan's hook test on OsteoShield packs three to five of
   these into every line, so the bank is the raw material. Mark which ones no ad in the market uses (Reclast,
   Evenity, Forteo, DEXA, "rescan in two years" on OsteoShield had zero ads).

8. **Ideas.** New since 2026-10-29. Ten or more one-line ideas, each born from a number on this page, written as
   "the idea, then the number that makes it true." Examples from OsteoShield:
   - The spine, not the hip: she broke her spine 194 times to the hip's 70, and nobody advertises the spine.
   - "Scared" beats "terrified" five to one; the ads all say terrified.
   - The paradox nobody runs: women with low density who never break. It is the VSL's best line and in zero ads.
   - She still works (332 posts): the first bone ad for a woman with a job.
   - Four injections she knows by name and has never seen in an ad.
   - Her mother is the number one thing she blames (202). The ad can be about the mother.
   - The compare image she is already googling: her bone next to a normal bone.
   Rank them: the ones with the biggest count and the emptiest market go first. These are options with evidence,
   not orders; Ryan picks. They are INTERNAL, the client never sees this section.

9. **Learning lines everywhere.** Every table, every ranking, every card ends in one plain sentence that says what
   it means. A number with no learning under it is a failed element. The learning is a reading of the data
   ("the 70s buyer is on Facebook, not Reddit"), never an instruction.

## Rules that still hold on the page

- Report, do not prescribe (checklist 36). The Ideas section is the one place an idea lives, and each one carries
  its number.
- Funnel-stage filter (38): nothing a buyer meets after the video, except the client flags at the bottom.
- Two quotes are not a fact. Every pattern states its count.
- Quotes are data. Never edit her words.
- No em dashes. No tabs, no collapsibles that hide the read: one scroll, top to bottom.
- Phone test: Ryan reads section 1 and the gaps table in two minutes on a phone.
