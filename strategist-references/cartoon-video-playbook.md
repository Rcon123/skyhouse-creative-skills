# Cartoon-Style Video Ads: The Playbook (health + beauty)

Date: 2026-07-28. Source: Atria API sweep of all 50 of Ryan's followed brands (50 newest video ads each, 2,355 video ads total, every preview still visually classified) plus Ryan's saved *Videos board (41 ads). 209 stills flagged cartoon/illustrated across 78 distinct creatives. 18 top creatives downloaded, frame-stripped, and fully transcribed. Raw pulls, videos, transcripts, and contact sheets in the session scratchpad (`atria/`); re-pull IDs at the bottom.

Scale signal = copy count within the 50-ad sample (variants of one creative a brand is actively paying to run). start_date is unreliable for longevity; volume is the money signal.

## 2. The seven visual lanes (all live and spending)

1. **Full Pixar-character world.** Whole ad is 3D-animated characters. EHF's two blob men on a couch (x10 active, 93s); Voraya's pomegranate-head woman (x5, 169s); Better sleep's glamorous 3D couple (Ryan board save); Cardiology News slice-of-life walks. Faces are charming, bodies expressive, zero AI-person uncanny tell because nothing pretends to be real.
2. **Toy world (LEGO/claymation).** EHF runs a LEGO-figure variant of the same beetroot script (x6, 174s): LEGO grandpa, LEGO Superman gag for "you're not superhuman," LEGO bedroom. Toy world lets the crudest VO in the market play as comedy.
3. **Villain personified.** The mechanism or the incumbent product becomes a character. Peak execution: Rosabella's Lisinopril bottle with an evil face delivering a first-person monologue (x4, 312s). Herbal Journal renders the lymph clog as a cute yellow gunk-blob waving HI from inside 3D anatomy. Live Anabolic personifies aromatase as "the gender-confusion enzyme."
4. **Comic-book metaphor universe.** Menerals "Reclaim Your Edge" (x4, 70s): blue-monochrome graphic-novel world where the body is an electrical panel, grandfather runs on a full grid, you run on backup power. The argument IS the drawn world (same DNA as the KO labeled-object statics, in motion).
5. **Mechanism overlay on live story.** The biggest volume lane. Live-action confession or demo, then drawn/3D anatomy takes over for the mechanism beat: Better sleep's collapsing airway (x17, 366s), Vital BP's Y-trap artery (x8, 246s), Emma's coiled colon and cork (x4), Daily Skincare's skin-layer cross-section (x8, 15-min VSL). Cartoon does the BBQ-test job: it makes the invisible internal mechanism visible in one glance.
6. **Cartoon mascot narrator composited on real footage.** A small 3D doctor/character stands beside real photos or real faces and narrates (Better sleep doctor x4; the Dr. Kelly serum ads Ryan saved twice). Cheap to produce, keeps real-face proof while adding charm and pattern interrupt.
7. **Illustrated ranking/listicle.** Smarter Reviews' liver torsos with five products "Tested & Ranked" (x11, 9s loops); Dr. Kelly's 3D-animated doctor scoring HA serums 1 to 10 (Ryan board save). The cartoon reviewer can trash competitors by name with less legal/social friction than a human face.

Craft sub-lanes seen live: kraft-paper cutout stop-motion (Cardiology News), handheld whiteboard mechanism lecture (Alicia Darling, x2 on Ryan's board), 2D illustrated avatar self ("Me at 30 vs after 40," Happy Mammoth board save).

## 3. The copy skeletons (transcribed, structure only, never the words)

**S1. Two-buddies confession dialogue** (EHF blob couch, x10 active). The single best cartoon-native skeleton:
open on the unsayable question between friends ("How the hell are you getting hard at 62?") -> raw confession of the failure scene, wife detail included -> wrong-explanation offered ("Because I'm old?") and rejected ("No man.") -> one-molecule mechanism in plain speech -> familiar fix killed with a reason (blue pills force a signal that wears off) -> the ingredient, with a purity trap ("Amazon beetroot is heat processed. Dead powder. Useless. Scam.") -> named product, spec line, proof counts -> benefit montage in his words ("Morning wood came back by week two. She started reaching for me again.") -> CTA + guarantee as challenge.
Why cartoon: two blob men can say what two real men on camera never would. The cartoon face removes the shame from taboo categories (ED, poop, man boobs) AND removes the AI-actor tell.

**S2. Villain monologue POV** (Lisinopril bottle, x4). The incumbent speaks in first person:
symptom roll-call, each claimed with "That's me." / "Me." / "All me. You're welcome." -> "here's what I actually do inside your body, and honestly it's not that impressive" -> the real mechanism it never fixes (nitric oxide decline) -> the trap escalation (higher dose, second med) -> "You're being managed, and managed is a nice word for trapped." -> the natural fix enters as the villain's feared rival.
This is blame-transfer-as-theater: absolution (it is the drug, not you) with total watchability. Transfers to any category with a resented incumbent: statins, laxatives, moisturizers, CPAP, retinol.

**S3. Socratic skit / podcast dialogue** (Live Anabolic x11 active, 259s; BHMD x5 skit; Emma's rapid-fire open). A curious skeptic feeds questions to an authority who lands the mechanism in speech: "Aromatase? What do you mean?" Rapid wrong-answer opens belong here too: "Fiber? Nope. More water? Nope. Laxatives? Nope. Here's what actually works." The learner voices every objection so the viewer never has to.

**S4. Day-by-day second-person projection** (EHF LEGO x6). "Day one, you swallow two capsules... you feel scammed as fuck. After one week, shit gets weird. Morning wood is back... By day 60..." Future-paces the exact buyer timeline, pre-frames the no-result window as expected (kills refunds), escalates benefits weekly. Same device as the Week 1-4 morning-wood timeline already filed in gethard intel; the cartoon version runs harder because the visuals play it straight.

**S5. Personified-ingredients fable** (Voraya x5, 169s). Literary storybook VSL for women 50+: the wound story first (hysterectomy, three years of "be patient with your body") -> each herb arrives as a woman with a personality ("Ashwagandha came next and sat down like an old friend... Moringa arrived with her hands full") -> heritage proof beats (Caribbean grandmothers, Tutankhamun's tomb, "handles everything but death") -> transformation with dignity ("She didn't become the woman she was before. She became the one who overcame it.") -> dropper ritual + sublingual mechanism. Emotional register no talking-head can hit; the cartoon carries grief and hope without a real face acting it.

**S6. Named-villain anatomy lecture** (Vital BP x8; Emma x4). Rename a real anatomical feature as proprietary menace: the left anterior descending artery becomes "the Y-trap... I call it the Widowmaker"; the female colon becomes "the coiled colon" with "a cork wedged in these loops." Then: everyday metaphor (rusty clogged pipe, traffic jam), authority citations (Mayo, Cleveland), standard-fix kills with mechanism reasons (fiber bulks the cork and makes it bigger), doctor-discoverer intro, 7-second-method curiosity CTA ("You won't have to put in your email or anything"). The cartoon diagram IS the nickname's proof.

**S7. Story confession with mechanism insert** (Better sleep x17 active, 366s). Highest-volume creative in the entire sweep: first-person stakes story (job + fiancee nearly lost at 28) -> tried-everything graveyard -> rock-bottom scene (3am sobbing, packed bags) -> doctor visit where the mechanism lands as a drawn scan (jaw falls back, airway collapses, brain panics hundreds of times a night) -> consequence escalation (dementia, heart attack, stroke) -> alternative dismissals with reasons (CPAP = managed not fixed; $2,000 custom MAD = price anchor; Amazon boil-and-bite = quality anchor) -> product as the third door -> silence-on-night-one payoff -> social ripple (friends ask, jealous sister) -> 350,000 sold, 53% off, guarantee.

**S8. Cast roll-call / organ-POV** (found in the final sweep batch; the personification device at maximum). Two flavors:
(a) *Organ-POV*: the body part itself narrates. Dr. Dave Brennan's "Hey, I'm your mushroom" (x9, 134s): the organ diagnoses its own three root causes, walks the 5-ingredient stack from its own point of view ("That's the raw fuel I've been missing... I get harder, and I don't quit halfway through"), and closes with a dare: "Are you going to fix me, or keep pretending it's just stress?" The euphemism ("your mushroom") is itself the shame license.
(b) *Full cast roll-call*: Thyroid Health Daily's "Meet the Villain Your Doctor Never Tested For" (177s): every molecule in the story introduces itself in sequence with "Hi. I'm X." Metabolism (wronged hero), Reverse T3 (villain: "I'm a broken key. I look exactly like the real key... but I jam the lock"), then the failed incumbents confess (GLP-1: "I just paused you. It was never my job to fix him."; Levothyroxine: "The higher my dose, the more broken keys he made"), then each ingredient-hero states its one job in the shared metaphor (Selenium forges real keys, Ashwagandha shuts the factory, Zinc pulls jammed keys). One metaphor system (lock and key), every character speaks inside it. This is S2 scaled to an ensemble, and it is the most sophisticated copy structure in the entire sweep.

Also confirmed at x6 active: the kraft-paper craft timeline (Cardiology News, 53s): "Day 1-7 CoQ10... Day 7-30 Nattokinase... Day 90: this is what a supported heart looks like." The S4 projection skeleton rendered as paper-cutout stop-motion, ending in a 3-SKU bundle ("Three supplements. One protocol.").

## 4. Cross-cutting copy laws (what makes cartoon video copy win)

1. **The cartoon exists to visualize a mechanism.** Every scaled winner is a mechanism ad; the animation is the BBQ-test made visible. No winner uses cartoon as mere decoration.
2. **Shame license.** The strongest cartoon categories are the unsayable ones: ED, constipation, snoring, man boobs, vaginal odor. Animated characters give the viewer plausible deniability and give the brand compliance/social headroom. The crudest VO in the sweep (EHF) rides the most family-friendly visual (LEGO), and that contrast is the engine.
3. **Nickname the villain, always:** Y-trap, the cork, the coiled colon, the gender-confusion enzyme, feminizing foods, dead powder, the Man Boob Enzyme pattern all over again. The drawn object carries the nickname.
4. **Kill the familiar fixes by mechanism, in a stack**, before the reveal: strips/tape/pillows, fiber/water/laxatives, blue pills, statins, creams. Each kill has a one-line reason, never a bare "doesn't work."
5. **Anchor against the expensive medical alternative:** $2,000 mouthpiece, $600 filler cycles, $300 luxury serum, TRT clinics. The product enters as the third door: not ignore-it, not the machine/needle/prescription.
6. **Spec-line purity beat** as differentiation: "cold processed, 1,300mg, single ingredient, no fillers, third-party lab verified, made in USA." The competitor version is "heat processed dead powder."
7. **Numeric proof stack:** 350,000 customers, 2.4M women, 18,000 Amazon reviews, 4.4 Trustpilot, 3M+ buyers. Cartoon needs harder numbers because there is no real face to trust.
8. **Guarantee thrown as challenge:** "If nothing comes back, you pay nothing." 30 to 90 nights.
9. **Karaoke captions, one accent word** (yellow/gold) per line, present in every high-volume cartoon creative. The ad must play on mute.
10. **Dialogue beats narration** in the cartoon lane specifically: 5 of the 7 skeletons are two voices. Characters need someone to talk to; monologue is reserved for the villain device.
11. **Awareness placement:** nearly everything here is problem-aware to solution-aware education. Cartoon video is a mid-funnel mechanism weapon, not a product-aware promo format (the one product-aware exception: illustrated ranking listicles).
12. **Length runs long:** the scaled winners sit at 90 to 370 seconds. Cartoon holds attention long enough to do full VSL work in-feed.

## 5. Beauty-specific read

Full-cartoon storytelling is rarer in beauty and skews to: (a) mechanism diagrams under real demos (skin layers, collagen fibers, giant-eye renders), (b) the mascot narrator beside real before/afters (Dr. Kelly, saved twice by Ryan), (c) illustrated serum-ranking listicles (score the category 3/10, 5/10, 6/10, then 10/10 for ours, with a mechanism reason per score: "one molecular weight sits on the surface... pouring water into a leaky bucket"). Beauty proof (faces, befores, afters, textures) stays photoreal; cartoon carries the WHY, photoreal carries the RESULT. Voraya shows the exception: for emotional women's-health stories adjacent to beauty (hormones, hair, post-surgery), the full Pixar fable works at scale.

## 6. Transfer plays for our offers

- **MOFO:** the two-buddies blob dialogue is a native fit for man boobs / morning wood (the shame license is the whole point), and the villain-monologue device fits "TRT/blue pills speaking." The estrogen siphon static already graded in is this same grammar; animate it. The Day 1/Week 6 projection matches the graded timeline recipe.
- **TPS:** the ranking-listicle skeleton (S7 scoring) IS the TPS Top-5 funnel model in video form; the Dr. Kelly HA-ranking ad is the direct swipe. The Voraya fable register matches the 65+ buyer Ryan's creature statics already target, and the AG-style stylized-character rules (dignified before, charming, full-body 3D) carry straight into motion.
- **Production note:** these are AI-generated animation pipelines (visible in consistency drift between shots). Karaoke captions + AI VO + shot-per-beat image-to-video is the build pattern; the copy skeleton is the moat, not the animation quality.
