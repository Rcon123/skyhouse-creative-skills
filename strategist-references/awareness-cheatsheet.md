# Awareness Level Cheat Sheet (Distilled)

> Source: "Awareness Level Cheat Sheet" (17-page swipe doc, saved verbatim at
> `strategist-references/source/Awareness Level Cheat Sheet (1).pdf`).
> Proven ad script patterns for each of the five awareness stops. Use when speccing
> hook direction and structure for a batch item at a given awareness level.

---

## BUCKET 1: UNAWARE (surface what's hidden)

The reader does not know they have a problem. The ad manufactures the problem in their head before ever mentioning a fix. Curiosity in, problem out.

**Pattern: Paradoxical Question.** Two truths that should not coexist, and the reader must resolve them.
- "How are ancient teeth, buried for thousands of years, perfectly preserved... while ours rot even though we brush twice a day?"
- "Why can children learn languages effortlessly while adults struggle?"
- Structure (Ancient Teeth): paradox hook > evidence stacking (no toothbrushes, no dentists, no cavities vs $50B/year and half of us with gum disease) > "something doesn't add up" > the reveal (a protective microbiome we destroyed) > blame transfer (not lazy, we sterilized our defense system) > soft CTA (no email, just a breakdown).

**Pattern: Trojan-horse listicle.** You think you are getting food facts, you are being walked to "your metabolic type is the real reason nothing worked", landing on a quiz.
- Structure (Nuts video): rapid-fire counterintuitive facts (cashews are poison ivy family, oats are horse food) > credibility through specificity > pivot to the hidden mechanism (metabolic type) > zero-friction CTA (quiz, no email).

## BUCKET 2: PROBLEM AWARE (align with the problem)

They feel the symptom. Do not waste a second surfacing it. Grab the symptom, reframe the CAUSE, raise the stakes.

**Pattern: Symptom > new cause > enormous stakes.**
- Snoring/Dementia: "It's not your nose. It's not your weight. It's your jaw." Then the stakes: nightly oxygen starvation linked to dementia (Thatcher, Reagan, Churchill). Then mechanism in plain language, then why every known fix fails (nose strips are a joke, CPAP chains you to a machine), then the product as the mechanical fix, timeline of felt results (night one silence, week one brain fog lifts), testimonial stack, BOGO + guarantee + scarcity.

**Pattern: The lies you've been told.**
- Credit Score Lies: "Paying your bills on time raises your score, right? Wrong." Known symptom (stuck score), reframed cause (three lies the credit companies fed you), insider authority (5 years inside the industry), the trick + loophole framing, proof stack of user results, single CTA repeated.

## BUCKET 3: SOLUTION AWARE (align with YOUR solution)

They know fixes exist. Walk through the category and knock each one down until only your TYPE of approach is left standing. Sell the category, not the brand yet.

**Pattern: Elimination run.**
- Mouthpiece Comparison: mouth tape falls off (and is dangerous), cheap guards are bulky, nose strips treat the wrong body part, CPAP works but who wants a machine on their face... only one thing repositions the jaw. Then the affordable version of the gold-standard category (custom MADs cost $2,000, this Italian startup made it accessible), personal discovery story, testimonials, guarantee, launch discount.

**Pattern: Kill the old paradigm, install the approach.**
- NASA Hair Cap: "Accept it? Cover it up? Surgery? None of those work. Most dead follicles aren't dead, they're dormant." NASA-derived mechanism (photobiomodulation), clinical timeline, results compound, scarcity + 90-day guarantee.

## BUCKET 4: PRODUCT AWARE (win the comparison)

They already want the category. The whole ad is differentiation. No education about the problem.

**Pattern: Spec demonstration.**
- Status Earbuds: "99% of earbuds use one speaker. We use three." Show the actual driver, explain why it matters, stack modern features, price anchor against big brands.

**Pattern: Feature war + identity.**
- Rain Jacket: "This isn't your grandpappy's rain jacket." 10,000mm rating, taped zips, PFAS-free (competitors poison you), pack-away design, social proof, veterans program, 60% launch offer.

## BUCKET 5: MOST AWARE (remove friction)

Basically sold. No education at all. Clear the runway.

**Pattern: Stack + deal + deadline.**
- Mushroom Coffee: name the ingredients they already want, "seen it everywhere on TikTok", it tastes good, massive sale ends in two days, grab a bag.
- Noom GLP-1: "$149. Delivered in seven days. That's it." Price, speed, one differentiator (the off-ramp), done.

---

## Rules of thumb across buckets

- The colder the bucket, the longer the education and the less it can look like an ad. The warmer, the shorter and the more branded.
- Every script meets the reader at their stop and walks them exactly ONE step forward.
- Unaware and Most Aware are the two least-crowded buckets in almost every market. Gaps there are usually the highest-value cells on the map.
- These are patterns, not scripts to copy. Swap in the offer's own UMP/UMS, personas, and VOC.
