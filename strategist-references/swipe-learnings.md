## PROCESS RULES (hard, graded in live)

1. **Iterate surgically, never regenerate.** When Ryan grades a draft, keep every ungraded line word-for-word; fix only the flags. If a fix needs restructuring, ask first.
2. **Formulas, not sentences.** Study winning ads (his or competitors') for beat structure only. If a line could be diffed against the source, it's a copy and he'll catch it.
3. **Format first.** Before writing, identify the format (cold ad / retargeting AT / advertorial / video script / squeeze) and grep his corpus for 3-5 real examples of it. Cold ads sell the CLICK (product unnamed, mechanism teased); retargeting ATs name and sell the product; advertorials do the education. A cold ad that teaches is dead on arrival.
4. **Ads protect the lander's reveal.** Read the destination page first. If the lander holds its reveal (organs, for MOFO), ads sell the villain/mystery and never pre-announce it.
5. **Angle diversity within a set.** Every ad attacks from a different door. If two ads in a set share a villain framing or opening move, replace one.
6. **Gaps are hypotheses.** An uncontested cell can be gold or dead sand. Present gaps as options with the case for each; Ryan's taste decides. Never crown a lead bet off gap logic alone.
7. **Copy deliverables are plain text.** No HTML, no markup. Ryan owns the page. Send to designer = Google Doc via Drive MCP.
8. **Compliance:** felt benefits only, real testimonials only, no biomarker/disease claims. Survey-shaped proof and conditional guarantees are the safe proof shapes. No em dashes, ever.
8a. **The long-form transformation montage has a fixed anatomy (Ryan, 2026-08-05: "the one big thing missing is the actual transformation... you're missing a lot of important details").** Model: 07.09.26 TPS Hotter. The montage is 5-7 ESCALATING beats, roughly 40% of the ad: (1) progressive mirror timeline (week 1 small, week 2 real, week 4 dramatic), then (2) 3-4 SOCIAL scenes each with a NAMED mundane place and VERBATIM dialogue (Whole Foods stranger with the wedding-ring tan line asking for her number, coffee shop "have a beautiful day gorgeous", husband on the couch "god you're beautiful" out of nowhere), then (3) social-circle reactions (women stop her constantly, sister convinced she's lying), then (4) an identity-restoration close ("Three months ago I was invisible. Now I feel like I'm in my forties again"). Two thin beats = not a montage = graded fail.
8c. **TPS failed solutions are EXPENSIVE; mass retailers live on the SOLUTION side only (Ryan, 2026-08-05).** The lander's premise is economical brands beating expensive ones, so the failed-solutions beat uses $90-$200 department-store creams and serums, med spa quotes, Botox money. Target/Walmart NEVER appear as where a failed product came from; in his winners Target is where the WINNING brand sells out. Free-and-common failures (face yoga videos, drinking more water) are fine, cheap retail failures are not.
8d. **Social behavior in scenes must be what people actually do (Ryan, 2026-08-05: nobody asks "are you dying").** People whisper to each other, ask the husband if she's "okay", silently assume cancer, put her on the church prayer list. The dramatic beat comes from a REAL behavior at maximum voltage, never an invented direct question a stranger wouldn't voice.
8a2. **THE AD-TEXT DOC FORMAT, graded by Ryan's own edit pass (2026-08-05, Ozempic Face doc):** (1) labels are `H1:` / `H2:` (FINAL headline picks only, never an options menu) then `AT1:`...`ATn:`; no ad names, no wording notes, no tester tags, zero meta - clean copy only. (2) The hook is the BOLD first line of each AT, written in his headline typography: Title-Case on key nouns ("I got Fired from my Med-Spa for showing Women..."), ONE quoted term, hyphenated numerals ("10-years", "3-weeks"). (3) The doc ships ONLY the keepers: he cut 6 ads to his best 4 rather than fixing the weak ones - deliver a curated final set, not the full working file. (4) His ad-HL shape for a named-phenomenon angle: two-word phenomenon question + promise ('"Ozempic" Face? Here's the Top 5 Skincare Brands Ranked by Dermatologists'). (5) He splits paragraphs even harder than the mobile gate: any 2-sentence para breaks again where the scene shifts. (6) Blunt buyer words beat softened ones: "when I was Fat" replaced "at my heaviest"; "thinks" replaced "swears".
8b. **Never paraphrase the locked promise (Ryan, 2026-08-05).** The TPS advertorial sells the Top 5 SKINCARE BRANDS, never "creams": every image headline and every in-copy list reference says "skincare brands" / "the #1 brand". Derivative campaign headlines inherit the locked wording exactly; only the audience qualifier changes ("...for 'Ozempic Face'").
9. **Brand-drug words get an A/B twin (Ryan, 2026-08-05, GLP-1 session).** When a campaign leans on a drug brand name ("Ozempic"), always prepare TWO full variants of every deliverable: the brand-word version AND a safe-word version ("the weight-loss shots", "weight-loss shot face") because the brand word can spike CPMs or trigger rejections. Same rule for image headlines: render keeper formats in both wordings so the test is ready either way.
10. **The corpus gate is real (graded 2026-08-05, GLP-1 session: first draft graded "really bad").** Writing from the rules files alone, with only one corpus example read, produced trailer-hooks (setups that promise a story instead of detonating one) and a diffable lift (the "Carol sends me a ranking" line). The gates: (a) read Fire-Hooks-Swipe-File.md plus 3-5 full examples of the exact offer+format before writing; (b) name the docs read in the delivery message; (c) fire hooks are present-tense aftermaths, transformation done and the world reacting ("Look at my face now"), never setups; (c2) SETTINGS get the same verification as pains (Ryan caught "weight loss clinic" / "GLP-1 group" as invented): every place, group, or scene in a hook must be somewhere the buyer verifiably goes AND phrased in her words ("my Ozempic Facebook group", church, the med spa consult), never copywriter-plausible containers; (d) BODY LENGTH, corrected twice and now settled (Ryan, 2026-08-05, GLP-1 session): for TPS static story ads the standard is the LONG-FORM NATIVE STORY, 400-700 words, built exactly on native-story-framework.md (hook -> personal situation -> failed solutions -> trusted-source discovery + skepticism -> Top-5 article -> progressive timeline -> interpersonal drama -> soft P.S. CTA + reader dilemma). Ryan graded 60-110 word bodies "way too short... that's not how we write long-form story ads." The short urgent-command register (Grandma to Gorgeous AT style) exists in his corpus but is NOT what he wants for these; when in doubt on length, ask which register before writing.

## AD FORMAT RULES (Ryan's, from his examples + grading)

- **Structure:** problem -> mechanism -> transformation montage -> effortless solution ("one capsule a day does it") -> click tease with something SPECIFIC waiting.
- **The montage is mandatory,** roughly a third of the ad, in concrete sized pictures ("waking up with wood again, the kind they haven't had since their 20s", "their wives are asking what changed"). All-problem ads are autopsies; nobody clicks an autopsy.
- **Pains from the deepest rung** of the buyer brief (teared up at a movie and couldn't say why), never surface tiredness.
- **Mechanism passes the BBQ test:** explainable to a buddy at a barbecue. For MOFO, "girl hormones instead of man hormones" carries the load.
- **Two lengths per concept** (short 80-120w, long 150w+): Mars Men median 84w, PV median 175w, both scale.
- **Static ads pull only static models (Ryan, 2026-08-05, GLP-1 session).** TPS static/advertorial cold ads come in exactly TWO registers: FIRST-PERSON STORY (the woman telling her own aftermath: Hotter, Menopause, 4X4 Group Photo) or DOCTOR AD (insider/expert confession: Fired, 4X4 Doctor). When writing statics, never model hooks or bodies on video-script corpus docs (UGC granddaughter, before/after reveals, anything hinging on "look at this photo / now look at me"): camera-dependent lines die in text. A corpus doc is video if the title says UGC/video/Cartoon or the body is spoken beats with stage directions. The Fire-Hooks-Swipe-File mixes both; check each entry's source doc before borrowing its register.
- His formula classes (mechanism ad, big-idea reframe, story ad with emoji results + one innuendo of restraint, ancestral epic, diagnostic checklist, 3-reasons listicle, timeline AT): full anatomy in `ryan-voice.md` + `offers/mofo/inputs/ryan-example-ads.md`.

## ADVERTORIAL FORMAT RULES (Ryan's, from his man-boobs lander + grading)

1. Curiosity + benefit driven, framed around the PHENOMENON, never assumed reader scenes ("Why are more men getting X? What the hell changed?").
2. **Skim test:** headline + subheads alone carry the whole argument in order. One succinct line per subhead, specific claim not vibe. Numbered lists get mini-subheads, max one stat per point. HARD FORM (Ryan, 2026-07-23, "remember at all times"): every referent in a subhead must be defined by the headline or an earlier subhead, NEVER by body copy ("The Food That Flips the Valve Shut" fails if no prior subhead said what the valve is). No side concepts that raise "why are we talking about this?" (belly fat on a man-boobs page). Read the subhead chain aloud standalone before delivering.
3. **Hold the reveal:** mechanism source (organs) stays hidden until right before the product. The unique mechanism is an enzyme/deficiency/named villain, never "foods" or "nutrients."
4. Keep the mechanism ON TOPIC for the angle (sex-drive page: every mechanism line ends in desire).
5. **Product intro pattern:** educate generically, product name enters inside a mechanism point ("Bull testicle is the #1 ingredient in MOFO"), then "What's in Every Daily Dose," then "MOFO vs Everything You've Already Tried."
6. Mobile-first: one idea per line, no paragraph over ~3 phone lines.
7. Advertorial headlines = curiosity question or discovery/benefit promise. Scene lines are ad hooks, not advertorial headlines.
8. **RE-ANGLING A PROVEN LANDER = SWAP THE FRAME LINES, NEVER APPEND CLAUSES (Ryan, 2026-08-05, GLP-1 lander).** Bolt-ons read as edits: a parenthetical jammed into the headline or a comma-spliced 'Including...' in the promise line graded bad. The right move: swap the SUBJECT of the headline so the whole page reads through the angle's lens ('Your Face Has TWO Kinds of Wrinkles' -> 'Rapid Weight-Loss Gives Your Face TWO Kinds of Wrinkles'), weave 2-3 angle sentences into existing beats, and leave the locked promise line untouched. Total diff stays tiny; the FEEL shifts completely.
8b. **ADVERTORIAL HEADLINE SHAPE, graded in (Ryan, 2026-08-05, GLP-1 lander):** he picked 'Why "Ozempic Face" Is Making Millions of Women Look Years Older... and What to Do About It' over fix-teasing, scarcity, and stunned variants. The winning shape: WHY + named phenomenon + mass-scale pain + soft open loop ('and What to Do About It'). The headline stays a pure problem-explainer; the AND/PLUS lines beneath carry the Top 5 promise. Parenthetical tails on headlines graded bad.
8. **TOP-FIVE LAW (Ryan, hard rule, 2026-08-05): the #1 is NEVER revealed on the page.** The page counts down 5 to 2 and then teases: "Who took the #1 spot?" / "What's the #1 worst food for your liver?" The CLICK is the reveal (into the VSL on a blind funnel, into the product page on a sales funnel). A top five that names #1 on the page has spent its whole reason to click.
9. **NAMED MECHANISM GRAMMAR (Ryan, graded twice 2026-08-05; derived from his own winners).** The proven names: "Prostate Flush", "Prostate Reset", "Energy Switch", "Hair Re-growth Switch", "Man Boob Enzyme", "Shut-Off Valve", "Swelling Mode", "Liver Clog" (market), "Aromatase Trap" (competitor), "ketosis loophole". The grammar every one of them follows:
   **[the thing the reader cares about] + [a dead-simple machine word]**
   - Word 1 = the body part, symptom, or outcome the reader already owns (Prostate, Energy, Hair, Man Boob, Fat, Liver).
   - Word 2 = a machine/plumbing word that states the action or the failure (Flush, Reset, Switch, Valve, Clog, Mode, Enzyme, Trap, Leak, Drain).
   - The name locates the mechanism INSIDE the body and implies both what broke and what the fix does. Often takes a time stamp in the H1 ("30-Sec", "7-Second").
   Three graded failures that define the boundaries (all bile, all 2026-08-05): **"Bile Switch"** failed because word 1 was anatomy jargon the reader has to be taught ("I don't know what that means or why I should care"). **"Dish Soap"** failed because the name imported a kitchen object instead of naming a part of the body ("dish soap is very unrelated... not quite right"). **"Valve / Faucet / Mode"** failed because the machine word was an invented part bolted onto the mechanism instead of the mechanism itself ("they're not related to the bile idea"). In "Prostate Flush" the flush IS the mechanism; in "Liver Clog" the clog IS the failure; in "Man Boob Enzyme" the enzyme IS the culprit. The machine word must be the thing that is literally happening, not scaffolding around it. When the mechanism is a real obscure term, the discovery-term route works: qualify the real word with the benefit so it self-defines ("Fat-Burning Bile", same pattern as the market's "Liver Cholesterol" and the wild's "brown fat"). An analogy can EXPLAIN the mechanism inside the body copy, but never be the name.

## HEADLINE FORMULAS (mined from Ryan's own corpus H1s, 2026-08-05; use WITH hook-laws.md)

The recurring shapes in his real squeeze/ad headlines, each with a live example from his docs:
1. **Authority Stunned + nickname + parenthetical:** "Urologists Stunned by 7-Second 'Prostate Reset' (Try Tonight)" · "Doctors Stunned: Simple 'Fix' Regrows Women's Hair (Takes 30-Sec)"
2. **Symptom question + nickname fix:** "Can't Pee? This (30-Sec) 'Prostate-Flush' is Fixing Stream AND Erections" · "Weak Stream & Weak Erections? This 'Prostate' Flush has Urologists Stunned"
3. **Authority command:** "NBA Doctor: Can't Pee? Do (This) 30-Second 'Prostate Flush' Tonight" · "Dermatologist: If You Have Dark-Spots, Do (This) Immediately…"
4. **Retailer scarcity:** "Costco Can't Keep (This) 'Wrinkle' Eraser in-Stock" · "Sold-Out at Target"
5. **Enemy betrayal:** "The Worst thing you can do for an Enlarged Prostate is see a Urologist" · "Why Your Urologist Will Never Tell You This" · "Your Current Face-Wash is Aging You FASTER"
6. **Dual benefit AND:** "Fixes Stream AND Erections (No Surgery Required)"
7. **Spectacle watch:** "Watch Her Age-Spots 'Dissolve Like Sugar' (Dermatologists Stunned)"
8. **Contradiction pattern-break:** "Wrinkles? STOP Washing Your Face (Dermatologists Stunned)"
Devices riding on all of them: ONE quoted word (usually inside the nickname: "Prostate" Flush), ONE caps word max, a number or time stamp, and a parenthetical (Doctors Stunned / Try Tonight / Do This / See How). Qualify the reader in the first 3-5 words (hook-laws #2).
**ONE SENTENCE PER HEADLINE (Ryan, hard rule, 2026-08-05).** A headline is a single sentence, joined with an ellipsis or a parenthetical if it needs a second beat. Never two sentences. Secondary loops go in the PLUS:/AND: stack lines, not inside the H1 (and squeeze pages get NO stack; the H1 stands alone).
**CREDIBILITY OPENS THE HEADLINE (Ryan, 2026-08-05).** The authority anchor leads ("Award-Winning NYC Doctor..."), it does not trail in a parenthetical. Payoff must be present too ("what's in it for me"), stated plainly, not implied by the mechanism.
**NEVER SPEND THE REVEAL AFTER A COLON (Ryan, 2026-08-05).** A colon that pays off the tease inside the headline kills the curiosity ("One Thing You Don't: A Liver FLOODED With..."). The held thing stays held; the colon is only for the authority prefix ("NBA Doctor:").

## SQUEEZE PAGE FORMAT (from Ryan's own Drive corpus, 15+ docs studied 2026-07-23)

Source docs (Drive, title contains "squeeze"): ProtaFlo Squeeze + New Squeeze, DentPure x2, PrimeBiome x2, Lung Expand, Hair-Loss Trigger, Brown Powder, Pickle, Blood Test, Bible/Proof, Army, Facelift, Hollywood, Botox, Salt.

1. **Length:** 150-350 words. At classic length: one flowing read, no subheads. One idea per line, ellipses for pacing. When a squeeze runs longer (the MOFO no-VSL adaptation), add skim-test subheads (Ryan, 2026-07-23): headline + subheads alone carry the whole argument in order.
2. **Skeleton:** H1/H2 headline options -> Dr. byline + date -> (City) shock opener, one sentence -> authority credential in 1-2 lines -> negation stack ("It's not your age. Not bacteria. Not genetics.") -> hidden real cause TEASED with a named villain, never fully taught -> transformation montage in concrete pictures ("peeing like they're 25 again", "their wives say it's like having their husband back") -> "In this short (5-min) video..." CTA -> [Watch Video] button -> "Real People. Real Results." + comments.
3. **The headline nickname device:** the mechanism/method gets a quoted nickname, usually with a number or time attached: "7-Second 'Prostate Reset'", "Blue Antioxidant", "Salt-Hack", "(4-Sec) 'Pickle-Juice' Hack". Authority anchor + nickname + parenthetical urgency is the H1 formula.
4. **CTA variants:** watch-video button OR age/concern selector ("Click your age group for step-by-step instructions" / "Select your #1 concern"). The selector adds micro-commitment.
5. **Image slots described inline** for the designer: curiosity image with play button, napkin/thumbnail carrying a one-line hook ("Urologists can't explain why (this) works so fast").
6. **Docs often carry a "Lander Inspiration:" URL** at top pointing at the design model.
7. **MOFO adaptation (no VSL in funnel):** squeeze sells MOFO itself and clicks to lp001, so the product enters inside a mechanism point in the last third + guarantee thrown as challenge. Everything above the reveal keeps classic squeeze shape. Comments = REAL lp001 testimonials only.
8. **A squeeze is NOT a short advertorial (Ryan graded v1 "boring", 2026-07-23).** The failure mode: teaching the mechanism in flat educational prose. The squeeze register is curiosity + benefits: shock cross-reference opener (ProtaFlo: "caused by the same thing that gives women yeast-infections" -> MOFO: "grown by the same hormone that grows women's breasts"), mechanism TEASED with a nickname and a nobody-tests-for-it beat, the fix held unnamed as long as possible ("a food butchers throw in the trash"), and a benefit montage carrying ~40% of the body in concrete pictures. For MOFO, the ESTROGEN shock is the big lever: a female hormone is doing this to men.
9. **Subheads only after a real run of copy (Ryan, 2026-07-23).** Frequent subheads over tiny 2-3 line blocks look weird on the page. On a long squeeze: 3 subheads max, each following 100+ words. The opener never gets a subhead; the headline covers it. **Image placeholder goes directly UNDER each subheadline** (Ryan's advertorial pattern), described for the designer.
9a-doc. **GOOGLE DOC FORMAT for copy deliverables: THE EXACT SPEC (extracted from the raw HTML of Ryan's NiteHush doc after THREE formatting rejections, 2026-07-23. Do not approximate; use these numbers).**
   - Font Arial throughout. Paragraphs have ZERO margin/padding, line-height 1.15. ALL vertical space comes from literal blank 12pt paragraphs: exactly ONE between blocks, TWO after the headline block and after the byline. Never use Google heading styles (h1/h2 tags add their own margins = the "triple-spaced" fail); size text via font-size on plain paragraphs.
   - "Headline Option #1:" label: 10pt bold with YELLOW HIGHLIGHT, headline directly beneath on the next line (no blank between label and headline).
   - Headline: 23pt bold, with the category keyword or nickname phrase in RED (#ff0000). One red phrase per headline.
   - Byline: "Dr. ####" 10pt, date line 10pt gray (#434343), the two lines adjacent.
   - Body: 12pt, selective inline bolds on key sentences. Section subheads: 16pt bold plain text (his numbered listicle mini-subheads: 14pt bold).
   - Image/designer notes: 12pt bold CYAN (#00ffff) in brackets. CTA buttons: 16pt bold RED in brackets with an arrow ("[Watch Video →]"); repeat the button again AFTER the comments.
   - "Real People./Real Men. Real Results." 16pt bold. Comment names 12pt bold with the comment text on the line directly beneath (no blank inside the pair), one blank between pairs; replies prefixed "↳".
   - ZERO scaffolding: no meta title page, no "PAGE 1"/"BODY" labels, no compliance notes in the doc. Versions in one doc separated by a horizontal rule. Disclaimer 10pt italic gray. Title convention: "MM.DD.YY - BRAND - Thing".
   - Verification step is mandatory: after creating, download the doc back as HTML and check sizes/blanks survived. The markdown read_file_content view HIDES font sizes; never judge formatting from it.
9b. **MOFO man-boobs mechanism vocabulary LOCKED (Ryan, 2026-07-23 + his own 07.17.26 ad doc): the "Shut-Off Valve" + the "Man Boob Enzyme" + the 2-nutrient payoff.** His canonical chain (use his words, this order): man boobs are BREAST TISSUE not fat -> grown by estrogen your own body makes -> one enzyme, the "Man Boob Enzyme" (aromatase) -> "you can't burn off tissue that's being told to GROW" (kills the diet objection) -> "skinny guys get them too" -> enzyme runs wild when missing 2 nutrients, zinc + vitamin A, gone from food ~100 years -> put them back, enzyme shuts off, "the grow signal stops," "the chest starts flattening on its own" -> "5 seconds a day," "wives noticing in weeks," "deflate/deflating" is his verb.
9c. **VOICE SOURCE OF TRUTH: his own most-recent ads for the same offer + angle (Ryan, 2026-07-23, after a "not my voice" grade).** Before writing any page, search Drive for his newest docs on that angle (they can postdate the harvested corpus; the 07.17.26 man-boobs doc did). The pre-lander page is the ARTICLE his cold ads promise, so it must deliver the ads' teased payoff in the ads' own vocabulary ("tap below to see the two nutrients" -> the page names the two nutrients). Prescriptive is the bar: name the enzyme, name the nutrients, say exactly what happens in what order. Vague words ("compounds," "a food") where he has specific ones = not his voice.
10. **The is-this-even-true test kills romantic history claims (Ryan, 2026-07-23).** "Men 100 years ago never got man boobs" died because it can't be checked and reads invented. Safe shapes: published epidemiology (20% T decline since 1999), food history stated as food history (organs left the plate ~100 years ago), and user-report montages. If a line invites "do I fully believe you?", cut it before Ryan asks.
11. **Qualify by the PROBLEM, never by a prior action (Ryan, 2026-07-23).** "You lost the weight but the chest stayed" gates out every man who never dieted. The door is the symptom itself (the chest, the dribble, the spots); sub-experiences like diet failure become ONE validating beat inside the body ("men who diet watch everything shrink except... men who don't watch it grow"), covering both camps without shrinking the audience.
