# TESTING PROCESS — PARKED STEPS

Pulled out of the research doc 2026-08-03 because they happen AFTER research. Raw material for the testing/creative process doc when we build it. Approved wording, do not lose.

1. The research brief feeds the creative brief (copy and creative together, one brief). Fernando owns what it needs to look like.
2. Every ad gets an awareness level: unaware, problem aware, solution aware. Nothing product aware on a VSL offer. No product name, no bottle, no price, ever.
3. Statics first, then video. On a static: image first, then hook, then body.
4. Test the angle and the format separately. Same angle in 3 formats. Nothing spends = the angle is dead. One spends = the format was the difference.
5. Then 5 hooks on the winner. 5 truly different first-3-seconds, not text swaps.
6. Name every ad: persona, angle, format, awareness, hook number. About $1k per angle, then sort by CPA and read it.
7. 70% iterations, 30% new.
8. When an angle wins twice: same angle, new delivery. UGC becomes a podcast cut and a static. Then write the SOP.
