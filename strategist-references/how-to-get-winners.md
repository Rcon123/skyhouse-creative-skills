# How To Get Winners (The First Batch framework)

Source: `how-to-get-winners-first-batch.pdf` (Ryan, ingested 2026-08-04). Distilled
operating checklist. Use for any new-client first batch AND as the grading rubric for
existing accounts (what to iterate, how, and how to sequence risk).

## The two games

Every engagement is two campaigns at once: the ads target the market, everything else
targets the client. Strategy = can you find winners. Trust = does the client believe in
how you move. They pull against each other: strategy rewards bold swings, trust punishes
them early. The whole framework is one loop: win reliably enough to earn trust, spend
that trust on bigger swings, repeat.

## Step 1: The Read

Read the ACCOUNT for two things:
- **Patterns** (what's working): sort by spend, active ads, top performers per product.
  Look in messaging (concepts, angles, awareness levels) and creative (formats, styles,
  creators, editing). One winner is an anecdote; three winners sharing a trait is a
  pattern. Write the pattern as one plain sentence.
- **Gaps** (what's missing): segments/personas nobody targets, styles/formats absent
  (all-video accounts lack statics), awareness levels (middle is crowded; true unaware
  at the top and creative most-aware at the bottom are usually empty).

Weigh gaps with **chad logic**: how big, how big the prize, how urgent (zeitgeist), is
it genuinely novel, is it relevant to what's proven. Judgment, not formula.

Read the CLIENT: hungry for winners or creativity? How much patience? Relationship
context? Sophistication? Gut read: how much risk can this relationship absorb right now.

## Step 2: What you carry in

Over-delivery (meaningfully more than asked). A coherent narrated strategy (they judge
the system, not each ad; losers become data points inside a plan). Demonstrated skill.
Reliability (on time, proactive, clean).

**The surplus trick:** hired for 10, make 13. Paid-for ads skew safe; the surplus
carries the boldest swings. A surplus miss costs nothing they ordered; a surplus hit is
a winner from work they never paid for. Misses are free, hits are legendary.

## Step 3: Explore vs exploit

Exploit = squeeze proven winners (reliable, smaller, fast trust). Explore = new
territory (risky, big when it hits, where skill shows). The ratio depends on (1) how
easy you think new winners are to find (big obvious gaps = more explore) and (2) the
client read (impatient = exploit-heavy first).

## Step 4: The menu (difficulty = difficulty of a WIN, 1-10)

**Bankers (sure things):**
- Style/format swap on the top spender (1-2). Exact scaling script, new format only:
  talking head to b-roll+VO, creator A to creator B, video to static. Proven message +
  algorithm treats it as a new ad = new distribution. Always add fresh hooks.
- Segment/persona transplant (2-3). Winning angle aimed at an ignored segment. Only
  matters if the gap is real.
- Competitor swipe (2-3). Borrowed reliability, zero skill demo. Adapt, never clone.
  Filler that pays bills, never the story of the batch.

**Bridges (chef's picks):**
- Concept/angle remix (3-4). Concept = raw fact; angle = psychological frame. If the
  concept is winning, hold the fact and rotate frames (whistleblower, conspiracy,
  geographic, accidental discovery, countdown). If the angle is winning across several
  concepts, hold the frame and rotate facts. 1 winner = hunch, 3 = pattern. Hooks
  refresh on everything.
- Organic swipe (3-4). Viral organic translated to paid. Softer signal, judgment call.
- Bottom-of-funnel (4-5). Quiet big spenders, narrow space, low skill demo. Smart play:
  pair BOF messages with a format-gap swap.
- Internal vectors (5-6). The shared DNA across ALL winners, named in one sentence, then
  expressed in a net-new ad (not an iteration of any single winner). Hardest non-bet
  play, biggest differentiator. Present the pattern analysis alongside the ad.

**Bets (moonshots):**
- True net-new / unaware ads (6-8). 2-3 per batch, no more. Most miss; hits open new
  fronts (that is how accounts 2-3x). Aim them at the biggest gaps from the Read, never
  spray. Framed as hypotheses, a miss is documented learning.

## Step 5: Set the dial (13-ad batch)

- Conservative (impatient, winner-hungry, burned before): 8 bankers / 3 bridges / 2 bets
- Medium (going in blind, the default): 6-7 / 4 / 2-3
- Aggressive (patient, creativity-hungry, gets the game): 5 / 4 / 4

Less trust = more bankers. You can shift toward bets in batch two; you cannot un-lose
batch one. Bets live mostly in the surplus.

## Step 6: Run it

Tell the story before results exist (found, patterns, gaps, what each piece is for,
expectations: some built to win fast, some built to win big or miss, by design).
Attach a one-line hypothesis to every ad: what it is, why it should work, what a miss
teaches. Predicted winners read 10x better than unexplained ones; hypothesized losers
are learnings. Launch bankers first so a win hits the scoreboard while bets spend
through learning. Overcommunicate on a cadence. Never silent long enough for wondering.

## Step 7: Read the signal and adjust

- Format swap hits: creative was saturated, not messaging. Run every proven message
  through every format gap.
- Segment transplant hits: you validated a segment. Treat it as a mini-account and run
  the full remix machinery inside it.
- Remixes hit but swaps flat: messaging headroom, creative tapped. Shift to bridges.
- A bet hits: jackpot. Shift a full notch aggressive and immediately run banker-style
  iterations on the new winner. Explore becomes the new exploit.
- Nothing hits: lead with the learnings, narrate the adjustment. Losses + legible
  learning loop reads as competence. Losses + silence reads as failure.

Loop: winners feed the bankers, learnings feed the read, trust feeds the dial. After
3-4 cycles you hold the accumulated map, and the map is the moat.

**One-breath version:** read the account and client, carry in over-delivery + story +
skill + reliability, make more ads than asked, allocate bankers/bridges/bets on a dial
set by the client read, wrap every ad in a hypothesis and every batch in a story, launch
bankers first, follow the signal, adjust in the open, loop toward explore as trust
compounds.
