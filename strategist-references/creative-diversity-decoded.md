## The Point of Creative Diversity

Two jobs at once:
1. Work WITH the algorithm to reach a lot of different people.
2. Create hyper-relevant ads people feel were made specifically for them.

The method: build a MAP of all the possibilities, then identify gaps, new pockets, and new angles. Spread across the map on purpose instead of piling onto one point.

### Why the map is literal (Andromeda)

Meta reads the ENTIRE ad (visuals, copy, on-screen text, audio, even the funnel behind the link) and places it as one point in a giant space of meaning. The scroller is a point on the same map. Retrieval = Meta standing the user on their spot and grabbing the few thousand ads sitting closest. Consequences:

- If two of your ads are too similar, they sit on the same spot. Meta picks one and benches the other. Your own ads starve each other.
- Entire pockets of buyers never see you because everything you make lives in one corner.
- Creative is a lever that MOVES you on the map. Same message, different look = different people reached.

Do not obsess over exact mechanics. It is an algorithm game and a human game, and both serve one purpose: the RIGHT person, with the RIGHT thing.

## The Formula

```
MESSAGING x CREATIVE
MESSAGING = Segment x Awareness x Copy
Full: (Segment x Awareness x Copy) x Creative
```

- **Segment**: who you are talking to.
- **Awareness**: how much they already know (where they stand on the road).
- **Copy**: what you actually say (CASH, below).
- **Creative**: how it looks and feels (statics ladder + CASTS, below).

The combinations are effectively infinite (20 segments x 15 styles x 5 awareness levels before copy even enters). Therefore:

## The Gold-Coastline Rule (how to act on the map)

Gold is NOT spread evenly. Buyers bank together in specific pockets, certain Segment + Awareness + Copy + Creative combos that just hit. Three diggers:

1. **The random guy**: digs wherever. Occasional fluke winner, learns nothing, cannot repeat it.
2. **The nerd**: grids the whole coastline and digs every square. Perfect logic, no taste. Starves before reaching the good spots. DO NOT BE A NERD. The formula is not a checklist you complete.
3. **The third guy (be this)**: reads the terrain, builds a story about where the gold banks, digs there first, learns from every hole, adjusts the story, and keeps just enough randomness to stumble into pockets logic would miss.

Every empty cell on the map is NOT automatically work to do. A plan that tries to fill every box has failed the doctrine.

## Segments: WHAT / WHO / WHY

There are only three ways to cut a market. They are complete. No fourth question exists.

| Question | Name | Order |
|---|---|---|
| WHAT do they want? | Outcomes | first, the spine |
| WHO are they? | Demographics | second, the easy layer |
| WHY are they here, what is true now? | Facets | last, deepest and most powerful |

A segment = one Outcome x a Demographic x a Facet or two.

Two rules at EVERY layer:
- **Say-it test**: would a real customer say this out loud?
- **The gate**: only make a new slice if it needs a genuinely different ad. Same ad = one slice. Merge.

**Outcomes**: the end-state they are buying, a pain gone or a result wanted. Push past mechanism (nobody wants "collagen", they want fewer wrinkles). BUT read the market, not the rulebook: a mechanism can graduate into an outcome when the market lives in it ("my testosterone up"). Sort by WANT, never by TYPE (pest control sorted by want makes the mosquito appear in two rows: the scared mom and the guy who wants his deck back. Two ads that sorting by type hides). Aim for 4 to 7.

**Sub-outcomes (optional)**: cut a want finer through exactly five lenses. Type, Location, Function, Moment, Severity. Each want has ONE primary lens doing 80% of the work (skincare/pest lead Type, testosterone leads Function, sleep/energy lead Moment). Never use all five. Add a second lens only if a customer says it out loud AND it needs a different ad. Prevention lives at the tail of Moment.

**Demographics**: tickable from across the room. Five: **Buyer first, always** (who pays, and are they even the user), Gender, Age (life-stage beats numbers), Race/Ethnicity (only where it changes the product's job), Identity (solidified life facts). Crossing outcome x demographic blooms one want into several pockets (3pm crash x 20s = "too young to be this tired"; x 60s = "keep up with the grandkids"). Same gate.

**Facets**: what is going on inside. Five families, reach for the ones that light up the market:

| Family | Test | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| Belief | "I think ___ is true" | the argument. Echo a belief they already hold. NEVER install your own |
| Identity (tribe) | "I'm the kind of person who ___" | vocabulary + hard-nos |
| State | "right now I am ___ / I've tried ___" | the problem you lead with |
| Value | "what matters is ___" | the promise you emphasize |
| Occasion | "it changed when ___" | the why-now. The hardest thing in marketing, handed to you free |

**Stacking**: stack TWO facet families and you invent a pocket nobody targets (vegetarian x just had a baby). Three or more = one person in a Reddit thread, no volume. Gate: enough people sit in it AND it needs a different ad than either facet alone.

**The Belief trap**: write the CUSTOMER's belief ("my hormones are the problem"), never the marketer's ("science-backed ingredients"). You find beliefs. You do not install them.

## Personas

A segment is a coordinate. A persona is the person standing on that spot: name, voice, a specific complaint muttered in the mirror. You cannot write mind-reading copy for a coordinate.

Five personas is not your market, it is five points on a map that holds hundreds. You do not build them all. You PICK: big enough to be a real market, reachable, sitting on a pain sharp enough to act. Everyone else stays a coordinate, waiting.

A persona is a fuel tank for copy. Every field must load an ad:
- **Handle** (Postpartum Priya, GLP-1 Gina)
- **Coordinates** (Outcome x sub-slice x demographic x facet)
- **One-liner**
- **Pain, in HER words** (the lived version, not "low energy")
- **The promise she's chasing** (after-state plus who she gets to become)
- **What she already believes** (echo, never install)
- **Her constraints** (tried, won't do, can't afford)
- **Her actual language, VOC** (10 to 20 verbatim phrases from the wild. Quote verbatim, NEVER paraphrase)

VOC comes from unguarded rooms: Reddit and niche subs, 3-star retailer reviews (gold), YouTube/TikTok comments, private groups, Quora. Invented at a desk = a fantasy, not a persona. Test: a copywriter who has never heard of the market could open the sheet and write pain copy in the customer's voice.

### Naming convention (the feedback loop)

```
[OUTCOME]_[PERSONA]_[FACET]_[ANGLE]_[FORMAT]_[vNN]
e.g. WEIGHTLOSS_BellyPPmom_Occasion_PreBabyBody_UGC_v03
```
Underscores between fields, CamelCase inside a field, broad-to-narrow always, fixed vocabulary for stable fields (only the angle is freeform). Done right, the name itself tells you why a winner won, and sorting your files becomes strategy feedback, not filing.

## Awareness: the five stops on the road

Everyone who buys walks the same road:

| Stop | In their head | Your job |
|---|---|---|
| **Unaware** | doesn't know there's a problem | surface what's hidden |
| **Problem Aware** | feels the symptom, wrong or no story about the cause | align with the problem: name it, give it a cause and stakes |
| **Solution Aware** | knows a category of fix exists | align with YOUR solution: sell the right type of approach |
| **Product Aware** | comparing you vs the other guys | win the comparison |
| **Most Aware** | basically sold, just not clicking | remove friction, reason to act NOW |

Rules:
- Awareness is NOT a sticker on a forehead. It is where they are standing right now. Same human, walking.
- An ad is a VEHICLE: it picks someone up at their stop and walks them one step down the road.
- Persona = WHO (fixed). Awareness = WHERE (moving). They stack.
- A persona's situation sets her ENTRY POINT, not her ceiling. (Ozempic Olivia enters solution-aware because she is already on the drug, and still travels all the way to the discount.)
- **Narrow move** (persona-first): lock one person, build the whole ladder down her road. That is a funnel.
- **Broad move** (awareness-first): lock one stop, fan one message across many personas standing there. ("Your body is holding onto fat for a reason" catches Priya, Dave, and Olivia at once.)
- Neither is more correct. Knowing which one you are playing is most of the skill.

## Copy DNA: CASH

Concept. Angle. Style. Hook. Concept is the WHAT, Angle is the WAY IN, Style is the LOOK, Hook is the WORDS.

**Concept = a flat, mined fact.** Three mines: Avatar facts (their 2am truths), Market facts (what failed, what everyone believes, who they trust), Product facts (discovery, mechanism, proof). The tell: you can say it flat, with no villain, no feeling, no "you". Concepts are boring. That is correct.

**Angle = the psychological frame that makes someone CARE.** You are rarely giving new information. You are changing how they pay attention to information they could already get. One flat fact ("vitamin D deficiency is linked to depression") through five angles = five completely different ads (conspiracy, geographic, accidental discovery, countdown, enemy admission). The concept never moves. The angle does all the work. You have a handful of true concepts and nearly unlimited angles, so most creative firepower goes into ANGLES.

Six angle families:
- **Discovery**: new discovery, accidental discovery, lost ancient wisdom, missing piece, real root cause
- **Urgency**: breaking news, trend, future warning, deadline
- **Authority**: the expert, insider whistleblower, unlikely teacher, enemy admission
- **Conflict**: conspiracy, contrarian myth-bust, David vs Goliath, scandal, vindication
- **Story**: case study, journey, confession, redemption arc
- **Reframe**: the wrong question, "you were 90% there", "which type are you"

**Hook = concept + angle compressed into the fewest, sharpest words.** Patterns: weird facts, magic words, weird methods, incomplete loops. Hook comes FIRST in the workflow; nail it and body copy tends to write itself.

Raw material comes from **STORMING**: raid Swipes, Organic, Research, New styles, and your own bank of winners. Strip for parts, file onto master lists. You stop writing from a blank page.

## Creative

### Statics: the three-level ladder

**The one principle: the colder the traffic, the less it should look like an ad.**

| Level | Funnel | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Native** | top, cold | does not look like an ad at all. Stop the scroll before the brain registers marketing |
| **Template** | middle, warmer | makes an actual argument (comparison, before/after, "3 mistakes") |
| **Brand** | bottom, warmest | polished and branded. For warm traffic, polish signals "safe to hand over my card" |

Native maps to catching unaware/problem-aware. Brand maps to removing friction for most-aware. Same road, visual version.

**SCRAWLS** generates native concepts (visual twin of STORMING). Seven doors, opens mining other people, closes mining yourself:
S Swipe (competitors ONLY, longevity = money), C Copy-Derived (push it weirder, disconnected images often win), R Reptile Triggers (13 primal triggers; if it makes you flinch, keep it; a tasteful reptile trigger is a failed one), A Audience Language (their words made literal: "white-knuckling the toilet seat" becomes the image), W Wild Sourcing (real in-the-wild images, become style references for AI generation), L Loopback (YOUR winners only, dissect and expand elements, the flywheel), S Source (your own trained gut). Never cross S and L: Swipe = market exploration, Loopback = own-account exploitation.

Native batch math: 15 to 20 filtered concepts, reference image for each (never skip), 3 to 4 generations each, pick the 12 to 16 strongest.

### Video: CASTS

Conceit. Actor. Style. Terrain. Setup. NOT five equal dials: **Conceit is the PARENT**, the other four dress its stage.

- **Conceit** = the vehicle the message rides in on (talking head, skit, fake news, customer call, podcast, personification, fake FaceTime, mockumentary...). It is the HINGE between message and creative: the last decision about how you say it, the first about how you show it. Same concept as testimony (talking head), story (skit), or news (broadcast) = three different map coordinates.
- **Actor** = Role x Look. Roles (why we listen): Insider, Expert, Peer, Icon, Character. Look (rotates on any role): gender, age, ethnicity, vibe. Match look to segment for trust, mismatch for pattern interrupt. A shocking number of wins hide in this knob.
- **Style** = visual treatment (raw iPhone, glossy, VHS, Pixar, claymation...).
- **Terrain** = where (home, work, nature, stripped studio; cluttered = native, clean = premium).
- **Setup** = frame arrangement (split-screen, hook-stacking, captions, b-roll, pacing).

**Carry vs host test**: if removing the element collapses the argument, it was the Conceit. If the argument survives, it was Terrain or Setup.

**The factory moves**:
- Lock the CASTS, feed it messages (one rig, endless concepts through it).
- Lock the MESSAGE, spin the CASTS (one killer concept through ten rigs).
Never a blank page. You are turning knobs.

## Strategy: Map, Gaps, Bet, Decider

Strategy is decisions under constraints. Four moves in order:

**1. The Map**: everything you could make. Terrain to read, not a checklist.

**2. The Gaps**: lay your ACTUAL ads on the map. Key gaps to hunt:
- New segments (whole new outcome, or finer sub-slice)
- New awareness levels. HUGE: almost nobody makes truly UNAWARE ads, and almost nobody makes creative MOST-AWARE ads. Everyone jams the middle.
- Diverse creative styles nobody in the category runs
- Totally new messages/angles
Finding a still-relevant gap is what blows an account up. Blue ocean. Most of the biggest winners come from here.

**3. The Bet**: explore vs exploit.
- Explore = net-new ads into gaps.
- Exploit = squeeze winners via **CASHED** (Concept, Angle, Style, Hook, Editing, Demographic). Know the levers: style/demographic/format changes make the algorithm see a NEW ad and find new reach; a new hook on the same visual or an edit helps the human convert but buys zero new reach.
- Do both at once. Only exploit and you plateau (winners fatigue). Only explore and you have no momentum.

**4. The Decider**: judgment + taste.
- **Chad Logic (judgment)**: a few big, chunky, confident calls. Weigh urgency of pain, TAM, gap size (own account and competitor landscape), genuine newness (new wins even with mediocre copy), reachability, zeitgeist. Do not grind cells in a spreadsheet.
- **Taste (intuition)**: compressed experience, trained pattern recognition. Valid ONLY because the feedback loop exists (analyze winners and losers every cycle). Skip analysis and taste is just confident bias. Taste fails on regime change, so keep explicit judgment running beside it.

## Workflow notes (how generation actually runs)

- Hooks first, then body copy.
- Four pieces: **Bots** (fixed prompts, built once), **Primers** (copies of WINNING ads, not research dumps: the winner already contains everything the ad needs to convert, and silently carries the style and messaging that is working), **Briefs** (instructions for this piece: segment, mechanism/argument, guidelines, hooks), **Templates** (optional structure to pour into).
- 3 to 4 generation passes, pick, clean up. Manual hook editing is NOT optional.

## How the strategist tool applies this doctrine

1. The coverage grid in every PLAN.md IS the map: personas (rows) x five awareness levels (columns) x formats (cell tokens).
2. Gap analysis is mandatory and ranked, with Unaware and Most-Aware columns flagged loudest.
3. Batches are bets: an explicit explore/exploit split, every item traceable to a grid cell, specced as a CASH stack, named with the naming convention.
4. Filling every cell is failure. The third guy digs where the gold banks and keeps a weird hunch in every batch.
