# Winning Statics Study — Ryan's Atria Followings

Pulled 2026-08-05: all 20 followed brands, every static ranked by run length, top 47 (181-302 days of continuous spend) studied as contact sheets. Raw files + manifest preserved in the session scratchpad; re-pull with `order=most_active` per brand anytime.

## The headline finding

**Zero glossy 3D medical illustrations survived in 47 winners.** Not one premium cutaway, not one cinematic render, not one "AI-looking" image. The winning grammar is REAL and ARTLESS: iPhone photos, handwritten notes, real bodies, flat blocky text graphics. Glossy = ad = scroll past. Artless = native = read.

## The six winning families (by frequency in the top 47)

1. **Real-photo product-in-life** (Emma, Happy Mammoth, 40+): bottle on a kitchen counter with bananas, unboxing flat in a shipping box, product held up on a porch. iPhone light, imperfect framing. The timestamp-candid family.
2. **Handwriting on paper** (Happy Mammoth 181d x4, Emma 301d, KO 262d ACTIVE x4): sticky notes on boxes ("Pssst… want 20% off?"), a HANDWRITTEN GREEN NOTE held to camera ("you are not lazy, your serotonin pathways are messed up"), a note TAPED TO A CABINET addressed to a name ("SARAH - I found it. The article about the shots & the numbness. You weren't crazy. Read this now."). Handwriting is the single most repeated device across brands.
3. **Blocky text-chip graphics** (Beyond Alpha 302d, Emma, Menerals 202d): flat background, product center, thick-type headline ("MY WEIGHT WAS STUCK FOR 6 YEARS, THEN I LOST 2KG EVERY WEEK!"), small callout chips around it ("BLOCKS FAT CELLS FROM FORMING" / "MELTS BEER BELLY AWAY"), benefit checklists, timeline chips (DAY 1/7/14/28). Menerals runs typewriter-manifesto plain text ("$44 FOR 100 MINERALS… FOR MEN TOO BUSY TO GO TO THE HOSPITAL").
4. **Real visceral body parts** (40+ 293d: two hands, one crepey one smooth; the rash thigh; the bikini mirror selfie): the body IS the mechanism visual. No illustration needed.
5. **UGC/IG-native chrome** (40+ actives): story-poll overlays, "it's all over my feed 👆" captions, numbered how-do-I-know lists in reply bubbles, whiteboard plans on brick ("My 2026 Bikini Ready Skin Plan").
6. **Hand-drawn labeled diagram — exactly ONE in 47** (KO, "WHY GLP-1 'FLATNESS' HAPPENS"): marker-sketch brain with arrows and hand-lettered labels (Food/Dopamine → REWARD CENTER). The ONLY mechanism-explainer that wins is SKETCHED, labeled, educational — the KO journal style already in the format library. Never glossy.

## Rules this adds for our statics and lander images

- Mechanism explainers render as HAND-DRAWN LABELED SKETCHES (KO style), never 3D medical gloss.
- If it looks like it cost money, it dies. If it looks like a person made it in their kitchen, it runs.
- Handwriting is the highest-trust text treatment: sticky note, taped note, held note, whiteboard.
- Text chips (thick flat type, benefit callouts) are the approved way to label a graphic.
- Real body parts beat rendered bodies for the visceral beat.
- The addressed-note device ("SARAH - I found it… Read this now") is a proven VSL-traffic pusher: it sells the CLICK to content, not a product. Perfect squeeze-page thumb grammar.
