The Secret Most Women Never Learn: You Have Two Different Kinds of Wrinkles

This is the single most important thing I can teach you about your skin. Nearly every cream you've ever bought only treats one of these two types, which is exactly why your results always stall.

Type 1 are "dynamic" wrinkles. The fine lines around your eyes, lips, and forehead.

Every smile, squint, and laugh folds your skin into little grooves. Over the years, they get etched in for good.

Type 2 are "static" wrinkles. The folds along your cheeks, jaw, and neck.

They appear when collagen breaks down and gravity drags everything down, like a balloon slowly leaking air.
