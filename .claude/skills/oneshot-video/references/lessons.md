- After assembly the measured offset runs about one frame earlier than on the raw clips (still inside the pass window). If a final check ever fails by one frame, add 0.02 s to the talk in-point in `step_assemble` before rerolling anything.
- A REVIEW for "mouth moving in silence" on a line with a long pause (an ellipsis in the hook) was the mouth resting and then opening a beat before the next word. Natural. Pull six frames across the pause before rerolling one of these.

## 2026-10-15, TPS Two Wrinkles face-build (voice-only demo, bust + gloves)

- A casting reel that shows the voice-generation face confused Ryan ("why is this a video of a doctor?"). For voice-only formats, put the real visuals (or a black card) under casting takes; the face clip is only an audio source and should never be shown.
- Ryan's grade on v1 (1:57, 142 wpm): "really great start", but the voice was "unnaturally slow", the opening must be the most satisfying skin move (the jowl grab), and list copy must say "the top five" and "number one on that list".
- Pace direction in the VOICE paragraph moves Seedance 2 mini only a little (139 to 185 wpm on the same prompt). A pace floor plus the cut-off bump made it WORSE: a longer clip makes the model read slower to fill it (84 to 130 wpm on pass 3). So: no `min_wps` floor on Seedance; keep every take and promote the best (`takes` step: exact words, not cut off, then fastest); and for voice-only formats use `voice.tempo` (1.10 to 1.15, pitch-preserving) on the finished track. `tempo` refuses to run when any beat shows the speaker talking.
- The Carol anchor carried over as the doctor's voice passed Ryan's ear ("the voices are good"): one approved voice can be reused across characters of similar age when the face is never shown.
- The listening judge scored every doctor take 1 to 2, including the one Ryan approved. Do not run it as a gate on this format; its calibration does not cover clinical narration.
- Hook variants: render only beat b01 per variant project, symlink the shared clips in, copy the native and clips entries from the main state, then `takes --only b01`, `assemble`, `check`. About 90 credits a hook.
- Suno on Kie returns `data[].audio_url`, not `resultUrls`; the client now reads both. A 100 s instrumental "spa piano, no drums" bed cost 12 credits and sat under the voice at -26 dB.
- Whisper writes numbers as digits ("$190", "68-year-old's"); the word check now spells digits out before comparing, so "a hundred-and-ninety-dollar" matches "$190".
- "jowls" came out "jollos" on three takes until a `pronounce` hint ("JOWLZ, rhymes with owls") was added to the prompt; the script text itself never changes.
- **Ryan's one remaining note on v2: every word said right, no word said twice.** Whisper cannot hear a doubled word: it wrote "face" for a take that says "face face" and "put" for seven takes that all say "put put". Gemini 3.1 Pro on Kie, asked for a verbatim transcript with repeats kept, heard the doubles on every run (21 of 21 on the b05 takes, 0 of 9 on clean takes). `voice.second_ear: true` runs that check on every take and on every line of the finished track (`check`). Whisper remains the word-by-word check; Gemini is the repeat check.
- Whisper's time for the last word of a clip is unreliable both ways: "Gone." ended 0.8 s before the sound did, "apart" 0.5 s before. Cutting the master at whisper's end plus a short tail clipped word endings, and the whole-track audit then said "missing: gone". The cut now ends at the measured sound end (`_audio_speech_end`, 20 ms RMS frames over the clip's own floor) when that is within 0.5 s of whisper, and starts at the measured sound start. Sound that goes on more than 0.5 s after the last heard word is a QA fault (a repeat, a laugh or a breath whisper skipped): reroll it rather than guess.
- "cut off" is now measured the same way (sound reaching the clip's end), not from whisper's last-word time. A clean 10 s take had been marked cut off because whisper said its last word ended at 10.08.
- "skin care" heard for "skincare" is a spelling, not a fault: the audit merges adjacent tokens when their concatenation is a script token.
- The native prompt now ends "Say every word exactly once, in order, with no stutter, no repeated word and no false start." (added 10-15 after the doubled words above; measure its effect on the next build before trusting it).
- Take picking order is now: exact words (whisper plus second ear), not cut off, then the fastest read. A tight end is a smaller fault than a doubled word.

## 2026-10-16, Two Wrinkles round 3 (Ryan: "jowls" still wrong, the chest made it look like a standing woman, rub the skin before it changes)
- Transcribers cannot hear a mispronounced word: whisper and Gemini both wrote "jowls" for a take that says "joles". A phoneme recognizer can (`phonemes.py`, wav2vec2 espeak model, calibrated on macOS `say`: jowls = dʒ aʊ z, bowls = oʊ, jaws = a). `voice.phoneme_gate: {"jowls": ["aʊ"]}` fails any take whose word window lacks the vowel. Use it for any word a client or Ryan has flagged by ear.
- Pronunciation hints in the prompt did nothing for Seedance 2 mini ("JOWLZ, rhymes with owls" still read as "joles"); respelling the word in the prompt's dialogue line did. `voice.respell: {"jowls": "jaowls"}` swaps the spelling in the prompt only; script, captions and audits keep the real word. "jowels", "jouls", "jow-ls" and "jowls (rhymes with howls)" all failed; "jaowls" passed the phoneme gate.
- A bust with shoulders and chest read to a fresh viewer as a real standing woman. The head is now a cosmetology training head cut flat at the base of the neck on a black round base, with a folded towel and a serum bottle on the table; gpt-image-2 edits held the base, towel and bottle through the lines-smooth, lifted and after frames.
- "Rub the skin a bit before it changes": every transform shot's motion now opens with "first massages/rubs ... for a couple of seconds, nothing changing yet; then ..." and the shots were lengthened to 3 to 5 s by starting them on earlier words (b02: two shots instead of four, b03: the jowl shot starts at the beat's first word). Wan 2.7 followed the two-stage instruction on all four shots checked on a filmstrip.
- The phoneme recognizer needs context: on the finished track (tempo 1.15, a 0.36 s word) a window of the word plus 0.15 s read "ɑː" while the same word in a 1.2 s window read "aʊ", matching the raw take. Gate on the raw take (the pipeline does), and when checking a finished track give it at least a second of audio around the word.
- "jaowls" respell plus the phoneme gate: body line passed on take 2 of a 3-run loop, the H5 hook on take 6. Budget about 4 takes per flagged word.
